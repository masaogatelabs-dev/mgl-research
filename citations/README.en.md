## Version and revision practice

- Each publication records its ID, version, first publication date, update date, changes, and pinned links to earlier editions. Experiment and publication dates are separate fields.
- A source report's version and its public summary's version are recorded separately.
- This catalogue lists pinned editions and provides a current-document link. A revision adds an entry while preserving earlier rows and links.
- Additional computation checks, changed mathematical findings, corrections, and withdrawals are logged with the affected version, evidence, and reason.
- Revision records specify changes to the document and additions to mathematical verification separately.
