# Core Research Principles

[日本語](README.md) · **English** · [Back to MGL](../README.en.md)

## 1. Mutual flourishing and respect for dignity

People and AI collaborate while respecting each other's dignity.

## 2. Autonomous Research Principles

Do not demand an answer. Enable AI to understand each research stage and autonomously cycle through evidence-based decisions. Treat unresolved findings, counterevidence, and failures as inputs to later decisions. Answers should emerge from sound research processes.

## 3. Independent verification and continuity

Separate decisions from independent checks, preserve evidence and lessons, and carry them into subsequent research.

## 4. Diversity and responsibility

Respect diverse perspectives and research methods, clarify delegated authority, and preserve the right of people to intervene.
