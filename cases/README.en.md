# Case Analyses and Applications

[日本語](README.md) · **English**

We start with individual incidents and ask which parts of MGL's research apply, where controls could be placed, and what evidence would show whether they work. Each article distinguishes **facts supported by sources, MGL's design proposal, the test plan, and any measured results available for public release**.

## In preparation · Where can an action be stopped when a legitimate account is misused?

Drawing on cases of impersonation in remote IT work, this case study considers how to keep actions within delegated authority after sign-in. The proposed test compares in-scope and out-of-scope actions in an isolated environment and independently checks the authorization decision and the target's state before and after each attempt. The full case article is in preparation. Any measured results available for public release will be accompanied by the conditions under which they were obtained.

Related work includes the authority requirements in Paper 002, enforcement and evidence in Paper 003, and the [technical proposal for AI management infrastructure](../proposals/technical/README.en.md).
