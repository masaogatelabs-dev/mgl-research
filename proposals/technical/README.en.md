# Technical Proposal for an AI Management Infrastructure

[日本語](README.md) · **English**

| Document ID | Version | Document date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MGL-ASP-TEC-001 | v1.1 | 29 October 2026 |

The proposal separates what an AI can technically do from the authority it has been delegated. It checks the scope of redelegation, places authorization and enforcement for high-impact actions at a control point independent of the agent, and uses evidence to reconcile decisions with execution outcomes. It also covers suspension, review, and recovery through reauthorization.

This page summarizes a proposed reference design and verification plan. The effectiveness of the integrated design remains a subject for verification. The companion policy proposal explains the institutional rationale and allocation of responsibilities associated with shared IDs **P-01 through P-08**.
