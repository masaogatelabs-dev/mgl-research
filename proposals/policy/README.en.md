# Policy and Institutional Proposal for an AI-Native Society

| Document ID | Version | Document date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MGL-ASP-POL-001 | v1.1 | 29 October 2026 |

This proposal considers how to assign responsibilities among developers, providers, deploying organizations, standard-setting bodies, and independent evaluators when work is delegated to AI across organizational boundaries. It covers grants of authority and oversight, incident investigation and remedy, appeals, privacy, and the adoption burden on smaller organizations.

This page summarizes MGL's proposed institutional arrangements for discussion. The companion technical proposal covers the controls and verification plans associated with shared IDs **P-01 through P-08**.
