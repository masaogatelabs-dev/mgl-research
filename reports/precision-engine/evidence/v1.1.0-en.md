This table summarizes MGL's validation reports dated 2026-11-01 and the recorded timing measurements. Each entry identifies the tested version, conditions, and scope.

| Reported item | Version and conditions | Result and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regression against the 80-significant-digit baseline | v1.1.0-dev.2 on Linux x86-64, using 200 requests with results recorded in v1.0.0 | Results matched for all 200 requests in this regression set. |
