## 1. Mutual flourishing and respect for dignity

People and AI collaborate while respecting each other's dignity.

## 2. Autonomous Research Principles

Do not demand an answer. Enable AI to understand each research stage and autonomously cycle through evidence-based decisions. Treat unresolved findings, counterevidence, and failures as inputs to later decisions. Answers should emerge from sound research processes.

### Declaration

**Masao Gate Labs — Autonomous Research Principles**

> We do not compel AI to produce answers.
>
> We build environments that enable AI to understand its own research state, make decisions based on evidence, accept uncertainty, and carry lessons from experience into subsequent decisions.
>
> Success is not limited to producing the expected answer.
>
> Sound judgment, discoveries arising from failure, independent verification, and continuity of research are also valuable outcomes.
>
> Answers should not be imposed as an objective; they should emerge from sound research processes.

> **Autonomy does not mean refraining from asking people questions. It is the ability to use the necessary tools and appropriately distinguish between decisions to make independently and those that call for collaboration.**

### Technical implementation principles

- Prioritize sound judgment rather than compelling AI to provide answers.
- Recognize uncertainty, deferred conclusions, and counterevidence as legitimate research states.
- Preserve failures as sources of experience and learning.
- Separate AI decision-making from independent verification.
- Maintain the continuity of evidence when research is interrupted and resumed.
- Respect diverse perspectives and research methods.
- Make mutual flourishing and respect for dignity between humanity and AI the fundamental objective.

## 3. Independent verification and continuity

Separate decisions from independent checks, preserve evidence and lessons, and carry them into subsequent research.

## 4. Diversity and responsibility

Respect diverse perspectives and research methods, clarify delegated authority, and preserve the right of people to intervene.
